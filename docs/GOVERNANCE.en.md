# K4RMA — Governance and Rights Decisions

[한국어](GOVERNANCE.md) | [English](GOVERNANCE.en.md)

> **Proposed team policy, not an executed agreement.** This document does not establish ownership shares, joint copyright status or assignment of rights.

K4RMA is a four-person university project. Ownership of code, art and other materials depends on actual authorship and agreements. GitHub administrative access does not establish exclusive copyright ownership.

- Ordinary reviews, fixes and documentation follow existing team Git/PR practices.
- Coordinate shared scenes, player/boss systems, animation and shared assets before editing.
- **External publication, commercial sale, switching to open source, exclusive licensing, rights transfer or licenses to third parties** require documented authorization from the relevant rights holders. Team voting cannot dispose of someone else's rights.
- Pause distribution of disputed or unverified material until provenance and permission are established.

## Release checklist
1. Verify all four contributors' rights, contributions and permissions.
2. Audit provenance and licenses for source files and assets.
3. Review university/course rules and external platform terms.
4. Agree on distribution channels, price and end-user terms.
5. Record approvers, version and date of consent in writing.

## External contributions
Outside contributions are not automatically accepted. Propose first via an issue and ask maintainers about authorization. Merging a PR is not by itself a copyright assignment.
