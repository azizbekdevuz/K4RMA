## Important notes

### Tripo-generated player and guardian
They are not included in this starter ZIP. They were generated by the team/user in Tripo.
Keep the original Tripo export and any generation/project metadata. Before public/commercial
distribution, verify that the Tripo account/plan used for the final exported models grants the
rights needed for that distribution. For the university prototype, still record Tripo in the
project's asset/source list.

### Standout 7
The Samurai and Golem pages currently identify the asset license as CC0. Download from the
creator's pages rather than redistributing copies from an unknown mirror. Keep the license/page
snapshot or README that accompanies the download.

### Quaternius
Quaternius states the models are CC0 and may be modified. Universal Animation Library 2 has a
free portion and a larger source/member tier; use only files actually provided to you under the
free download unless the team purchases/receives the other tier.

### Unity packages
A `.unitypackage` can contain demo scenes, shaders and scripts in addition to art. Import into a
branch, inspect the import list, and avoid bringing unrelated project settings or sample content
into the final K4RMA repository.
