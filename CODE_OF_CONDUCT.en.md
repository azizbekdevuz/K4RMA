# K4RMA Code of Conduct

[한국어](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md)

K4RMA is a small collaborative game project. Treat others respectfully in issues, PRs, reviews, team chats, meetings and project spaces.

## Expected conduct
- Critique code and design with concrete evidence; do not attack people.
- Respect ownership and coordination of teammates' work; do not disclose private project material without permission.
- No harassment, discrimination, threats, repeated personal abuse, sexual misconduct or non-consensual disclosure of private information.
- Raise errors and disagreements constructively, without public humiliation.

## Reporting and enforcement
**Do not post identifying details of conduct complaints in public issues.** Use a safe, private channel agreed with a team maintainer or GitHub's applicable reporting features. Maintainers should respect confidentiality as far as practicable, recuse conflicted decision-makers, assess reports fairly and consider proportionate measures such as warnings, access restrictions or refusal of contributions. For immediate danger, seek appropriate local emergency assistance.

Report vulnerabilities under [SECURITY.en.md](SECURITY.en.md). This code of conduct does not alter copyright or outside-contribution permissions.
